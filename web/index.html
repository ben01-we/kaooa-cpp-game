How to play Kaooa

01 Place your flock

Crows start. Place one crow on an empty point. The vulture then enters. Alternate turns; place all seven crows before moving them.

02 Follow the star

Move to a connected empty point. The vulture must jump an adjacent crow when possible, landing on the next empty point in the same straight line. One capture per turn; crows cannot jump.

03 Trap or capture

Crows win by leaving the vulture no legal move. The vulture wins by capturing four crows. Three repetitions of a position produce a draw in this edition.
